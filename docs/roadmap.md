# Roadmap

Tickforge's first version (the exchange, the market, the terminal, the bot and the challenge) shipped in November 2026. This is the feature roadmap that follows it, built a day at a time. Each day lands as one working, tested commit.

## Tickforge — from 2026-11-27

- [x] **Day 1 — Strategy backtester in C++.** A `Strategy` interface that trades the user's account, a runner that warms a market up, hands over control for a fixed session and samples equity each second, and four built-in strategies (Avellaneda-Stoikov quoting, moving-average trend following, mean reversion, book imbalance) with passive or aggressive execution. Metrics a desk would ask for: P&L split into spread, inventory and fees, annualised Sharpe, max drawdown, maker share, markouts, t-statistic across seeds. `tf_backtest` command-line tool and 11 tests with hand-calculated metrics.
- [x] **Day 2 — Strategy Lab in the browser.** The backtester compiled into the WebAssembly module and run in a Web Worker. A full-screen lab with strategy picker and parameter controls, scenario, session count and length; an equity fan of every session with median and 10–90% band; a dot plot of where sessions finished; a P&L attribution chart; a leaderboard that runs every strategy on the same seeds.
- [x] **Day 3 — Replayable sessions.** Record a terminal session as its seed plus a timestamped log of the user's actions, replay it deterministically with a scrubber, share it as a link, and open any lab session in the terminal to watch the strategy trade it. Landed with a steppable `BacktestSession` shared by the lab and the terminal, exact-microsecond stepping, a scrubber with a full-session preview, take-over, and determinism tests in C++, in unit tests and end to end in a browser.
- [ ] **Day 4 — Trade-level P&L attribution.** Per-fill attribution by counterparty type, markout curves out to 60 seconds (the signature plot), and a waterfall of where each session's money came from, in both the terminal and the lab.
- [ ] **Day 5 — Latency.** Give the user's orders a configurable network latency and show what it costs a market maker racing the informed trader. Measure the engine's per-message latency in the browser and show a percentile histogram next to the native benchmark.
- [ ] **Day 6 — Parameter sweeps and overfitting.** A two-parameter heat map in the lab and a walk-forward split (fit on some seeds, test on others) that shows how an optimised strategy degrades out of sample.
- [ ] **Day 7 — Polish.** README and design notes for the new features with screenshots, tour steps for the lab, mobile pass, performance checks.
